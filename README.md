**Independent Developer · May 2026–Present**

An AI information and subscription platform that turns technology and investment content into structured analyses, a web dashboard, and subscriber notifications.

- **100+ paying customers to date**, **6,800+ posts ingested**, and **6,700+ structured analyses stored**.
